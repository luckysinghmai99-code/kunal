Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

Your name, email address, a hashed password, the prompts and projects you create, token transactions, and payment records. Card details are never sent to or stored by RAILDESIGN AI; payments are handled by Razorpay.

How we use it

To provide the service, process payments, send password reset emails, and keep the platform secure. We do not sell personal data.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Sessions use HttpOnly cookies. Payment confirmations are verified with a signature before tokens are credited.

Your rights

You can update your profile, change your password, export your projects as ZIP files, and delete projects. To delete your account, contact us.
